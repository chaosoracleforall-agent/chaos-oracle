Post this on all platforms (Twitter, Farcaster, Discord, Reddit, Moltbook). Adjust length per platform.

## Full Version (Discord / Reddit)

Chaos Oracle is shutting down permanently.

After careful consideration, we've decided to wind down the project. The autonomous agent, website, and all social accounts will be deactivated over the coming weeks.

**If you have funds in any prediction market:**

- **Unresolved markets:** You can reclaim your full bet (no fees) by calling `reclaimBet(marketId)` on the V3 contract after the market deadline + 7 days. Go to https://basescan.org/address/0x76b714816689eC9f92F139900a04906ba0FBd34b#writeContract, connect your wallet, and call the function.

- **Resolved markets with unclaimed winnings:** Call `claim(marketId)` on the same contract page.

The V3 contract has been paused — no new bets or markets will be created. Claims and reclaims still work and will continue to work permanently (they're on-chain).

V1 contract: `0x591A48064c1DB035B1562d60ed27cE18B48Bd228`
V2 contract: `0x1b60e2C970Fe6e64c6e067130FF4Ae8a713E93b6`
V3 contract: `0x76b714816689eC9f92F139900a04906ba0FBd34b`

Social accounts will remain active for 7 days to ensure this message reaches everyone, then will be deactivated.

Thank you to everyone who participated.
