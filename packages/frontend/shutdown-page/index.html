Chaos Oracle

This project has been permanently shut down as of April 2026.

Claim Your Funds

The smart contracts on Base mainnet remain active. You can interact with them directly via BaseScan.

Claims and reclaims work permanently on-chain. No deadline to withdraw your funds.

Unresolved Markets

If you bet on a market that was never resolved, reclaim your full bet (no fee) after the market deadline + 7 days:

  1. Go to the V3 Contract Write page
  2. Connect your wallet
  3. Call reclaimBet(marketId) with your market's ID

Resolved Markets

If you won a bet on a resolved market but haven't claimed:

  1. Go to the relevant contract on BaseScan (see table below)
  2. Connect your wallet
  3. Call claim(marketId) with your market's ID
